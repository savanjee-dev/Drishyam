Drishyam 2.7.16

Material 3 Expressive redesign preview

- Introduces a clearer editorial hierarchy on the Home feed.
- Restyles photo category filters as theme-aware Material 3 chips.
- Refreshes Settings with a tonal hero card and rounded Material 3 surface groups.
- Refines the photo viewer detail sheet with a large rounded Material surface while preserving the immersive photo canvas and existing controls.
- Continues to respect Dark Mode, saved content, likes, bookmarks, and existing preferences.
- Keeps optional motion restrained and gated by Low Effects and Android system animator settings.

This release is intended for owner device testing after automated build and APK signature verification. Please report visual, interaction, photo-loading, and upgrade issues for follow-up fixes.
