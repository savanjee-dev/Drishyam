Drishyam 2.6.0 — Complete UI redesign

DESIGN
• Introduces the Moss & Olive visual identity across light and dark themes.
• Adds a coordinated Home, Search, Favourites and Settings bottom navigation.
• Redesigns the Settings hub with an editorial hierarchy and rounded preference cards.
• Reimagines the About experience with an updated developer/story presentation.
• Refines glass surfaces, card shapes, colour contrast and selected navigation states.

EXPERIENCE
• Adds an accessible route from Settings to the About story.
• Keeps wallpaper actions inside the full-screen photo viewer.
• Retains System, Light and Dark theme preferences.
• Updates the What's New information for the redesign.

PRESERVATION
• The existing photo-loading and API implementation is intentionally retained.
• Existing saved photos, likes, bookmarks and settings are intended to remain intact.
• No change to package ID: com.savan.drishyam.

VERIFICATION
• This release must pass the automated Android build and APK signature checks before distribution.
• Real-device visual and interaction testing is still required after installation.
