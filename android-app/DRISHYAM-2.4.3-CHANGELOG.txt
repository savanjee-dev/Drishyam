Drishyam 2.4.3 — Immersive Photography Redesign

• Restored Drishyam branding with “by Savan”.
• Rebuilt the home feed around Unsplash-only photo discovery; Reddit and subreddit categories are removed from the home experience.
• Added a compact glass header and one horizontal row of curated categories: For You, Forest, Nature, Earth, Hiking, Mountains, Wildlife, Architecture, Abstract, Minimalism, Travel, Street, Ocean and Night Sky.
• Reduced the vertical footprint of the header and floating bottom navigation to give photographs more screen space.
• Added floating navigation that hides while scrolling down and returns as soon as upward scrolling begins.
• Redesigned the Settings icon and retained rich glass effects on interactive controls.
• Added subtle card entrance motion and refined transitions.
• Redesigned the About page with Savan Jee’s portrait, three supplied friendship photographs, and a heartfelt story featuring Akshat Jha as second developer and creative collaborator.
• Retained photo attribution, original photo links, favourites, saved items and wallpaper actions.
• Bumped version to 2.4.3 (versionCode 14).

Photos remain credited to their creators. Unsplash search requires the repository secret UNSPLASH_ACCESS_KEY at build time.
